ONE PLAYER, TWO CHATS

Play from Entropia

Link your avatar once. Your in-game commands then use your existing Twitch RPG character—no extra app to install.

  1. 1
    Start in PlayPointGames Twitch chat!avatar "Full Avatar Name"

    Replace the example with your complete Entropia avatar name. Keep the quotation marks.

  2. 2
    Confirm from that avatar in game!twitch "yourtwitchname"

    Join the Entropia ppg chat channel and send this from the exact avatar you requested. Use your Twitch username, not your PPG website nickname. There is no code or expiry timer.

  3. 3
    Play in either chat!mine

    After confirmation, use the RPG commands in Twitch chat or the selected game channel. Watch Twitch chat and the stream for replies and action results.

The game channel is currently ppg; the streamer can change it. If linking fails, check both names and the channel, or ask the admin to help. Each Twitch account can link to one avatar, and each avatar to one Twitch account.

GAME CHAT → TWITCH

Your game chat can join the stream

Messages and commands from the selected game channel can appear in Twitch chat like this:

Apielol said in game: !mine

The streamer can relay all messages, commands only, or turn the relay off. Turning the relay off does not disable linked game commands.

Replies appear on Twitch, not in Entropia. Mining and stealing also appear in the on-stream action queue. PPGBot does not send messages back into the game.

The bot must be online and the stream host must be receiving the selected game channel. Commands sent while the reader is stopped are not replayed later.

Same character

Your level, XP, gold, minerals, spins and Pickaxe uses stay together. Switching chats does not create a second player or reset progress.

Same timers

Mining and stealing share one 5-minute timer across both chats. Gambling has a separate 5-minute timer and a 500-gold maximum. For !steal @username, always use the victim’s Twitch username, even in game.

Same rewards

Shop purchases, spin redemption and giveaways use the same account. Entering from both chats still gives only one giveaway entry. Keep your PPG website nickname linked with !nick "YourPPGNickname" for giveaways and redemption.

Avatar link ≠ reward nickname. !avatar links Entropia to Twitch; !nick identifies your PlayPointGames website account. They are separate.

Chat logging: when enabled, the bot keeps separate local records of Twitch chat (including bot replies) and messages from the selected game channel for support. Game-channel messages may also be shared publicly through the Twitch relay.

A failed or cooldown-blocked mine does not use a charge. Stealing does not use charges. Buy another Pickaxe after all 10 uses are spent.

GIVEAWAYS

One entry. One winner.

When a giveaway is open, link your PPG nickname and spend 25 gold to enter. Each player can enter once—even when using both Twitch and game chat. One person is randomly picked when the giveaway closes.

!giveawayCheck status and rules
!enterPay 25 gold and enter

The current prize is 10 spins, added to the winner’s chat inventory.
