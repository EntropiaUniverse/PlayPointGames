THE CORE LOOP

Choose your next move

⛏️

Mine

!mine

Join the on-stream queue and uncover one random mineral. You also receive that mineral’s XP and gold reward.

🥷

Steal

!steal @username

You have a 40% chance to take one mineral, 10% of the victim’s gold and the mineral’s XP. If caught, you pay them 10% of your gold.

🎲

Gamble

!gamble 50

Bet 1–500 gold—or use !gamble all to bet up to 500. Win the same amount on rolls 51–100; lose the wager on rolls 1–50.

Two personal timers. Mining and stealing share a 5-minute cooldown. Gambling has its own separate 5-minute cooldown.

LEVEL 5 UPGRADE

Lucky Pickaxe

Reach level 5, then spend 100 gold with !buy pickaxe. Your next 10 completed mines have a better chance of finding minerals other than Stone. Check remaining uses with !profile.

A failed or cooldown-blocked mine does not use a charge. Stealing does not use charges. Buy another Pickaxe after all 10 uses are spent.
